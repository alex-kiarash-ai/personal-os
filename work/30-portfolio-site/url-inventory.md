## The comp-card QR: verified, not assumed

The relay flagged the QR target as UNVERIFIED. **Resolved 2026-08-03 by re-encoding, not by reading the filename.** `qr-shaheenkiarash-com.svg` was generated by `segno`; re-encoding candidate strings and byte-comparing the SVG path data gives an exact match at:

> **`https://shaheenkiarash.com`** - version 4, error correction H, border 2, scale 12.

So the QR points at the apex, which survives the rebuild untouched. **Printed comp cards are safe forever** and impose exactly one constraint: the apex must always serve a real page. No redirect needed.

## The intent pages deserve a decision, not a 301

The three `*-model-stockholm` pages were built deliberately (research run 31/32) as an SEO play, they are in the sitemap at priority 0.8, and the site's own indexing problem was a headline finding of that work. PROJECT.md's frozen v1 scope is four pages, so they drop out, and a 301 keeps them from 404ing.

**But the architecture makes them nearly free to bring back.** PROJECT.md's content model already carries an empty `tags` field on every photo, specifically so categories can be added later without restructuring. Commercial / fitness / hair ARE those categories. In Astro, a tag-driven category page is a content-collection filter plus one dynamic route: the same photos, no new content model, no restructure. That is the v1.1 candidate PROJECT.md's own structural rule was written to enable.

**Recommendation for Shaheen (not a decision Alex should take alone):** ship v1 at four pages with the 301s, then reinstate the three intent pages in v1.1 as tag-driven routes. Reinstating means the 301s come back out, so keep them in one config block, not scattered.
