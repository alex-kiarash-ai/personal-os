# 11 - WhatsApp Harvest (on-demand)

## What it actually does

**Run 2 (2026-09-02) changed what this project is for.** Run 1 was about refreshing dates. Run 2 answered a harder question Shaheen asked directly: for every person he talks to, who are they, how close are they, what did we plan together, and what did we agree that belongs in my calendar. 476,091 messages across 1,323 chats. The two things it found first were not insights about his friends: a payment card he had sent in plaintext to two chats, and the fact that his best friend and Alex collaborator existed in the vault twice under two names, which had left him flagged as a possible attacker for three weeks. Both were sitting in data the system already had and had never read.

On demand since 2026-07-10, when the first Phase 2 harvest worked: it reads a local *encrypted iPhone backup* - no UI automation, no hacked APIs, zero ban risk - whenever a fresh backup is worth ingesting. The original Phase 1 approach (screen capture of the official desktop client, **paused 2026-06-18** and since retired as a dead end) ran at 02:30 while Shaheen slept. Text only, never media, and since 2026-09-02 that is true **by construction rather than by cleanup**: only three small database files come out of the backup, so no media directory is ever created. **Voice notes are now transcribed too** (Shaheen's call, 2026-09-02, reversing the old ignore rule): locally, nothing leaving the machine, each audio file deleted the moment it has been read. That change mattered because the three people he speaks to most send more voice notes than messages, so the old text-only rule left his closest relationships half blank. It harvests two things: Shaheen's own messages (into soul.md's per-language voice registers - his Swedish, English, Arabic, Farsi registers all differ) and context about friends (into their vault people pages - life events, not transcripts). It also flags personal messages left unanswered for 48+ hours to the morning brief.

## Why it exists
The voice corpus is the foundation every draft-writing automation stands on, and nowhere is Shaheen's *real* voice more honest than WhatsApp. The people harvest keeps relationships accurate without manual note-taking. Phase 1 was paused for a boring reason: the screen-capture approach ate AI tokens too fast, and the budget rule says captured-data quality never justifies burning the month's limit. **Proven 2026-07-10:** Phase 2 (reading from an encrypted iPhone backup instead - cheap, complete, zero ban risk) ran successfully the same day it was committed - 461k messages processed, real voice-corpus lines harvested, and the CRM's real last-contact dates + channel filled in. A live WhatsApp feed (a read-only WAHA gateway on the server) is built but deliberately switched OFF until Shaheen lands a role, because an always-on unofficial client carries a small-but-real account-ban risk his family ties can't afford mid-hunt.

## Works together with
- **soul.md** - the primary customer: voice registers per language.
- **[Personal CRM](05-personal-crm.md)** and vault people pages - friend context + warmth signals; the harvest writes each contact's channel + real last-contact so the CRM stops guessing WhatsApp recency.
- **[Morning Brief](02-morning-brief.md)** - the 48-hour unanswered flags.
